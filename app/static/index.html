How it works

Four steps from topic to finished video

  1. 01

    Script

    An LLM writes the hook, the full script and a structured scene plan.

  2. 02

    Generate

    Visuals and voiceover are produced for each scene.

  3. 03

    Compose

    Scenes, audio and captions become a 9:16 MP4.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Automated checks on format, duration and completeness.

Structured story engine
