01 · PROBLEM + GAP

The feed is hungry. Nobody has time to cook.

Creators

Have the knowledge. Not the editing hours.

Platform

Needs more video than people can make.

Users

Want signal from people they trust.

WHY CURRENT TOOLS FALL SHORT

Runway, Pika and CapCut are strong at clips. Each one still needs a human at every step: prompt, cut, caption, check, export.

There is no repeatable system that turns one topic into a finished vertical video.

02 · THE PIPELINE

Four stages. Zero hand-offs.

  1. 01

    Script

    A structured scene plan: hook, scenes, narration and a visual prompt for every scene.

  2. 02

    Generate

    Visuals and voice are made scene by scene, in parallel. If a source fails, a fallback steps in.

  3. 03

    Compose

    Scenes cut to the narration, captions synced to the words, framed for 9:16.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Automated checks run before any video is marked ready.

04 · THE QUALITY GATE

Nothing ships until it passes.

Every video is probed before it is marked ready. A fail comes with a reason, not a shrug.

  • File is not empty
  • Video stream present
  • 9:16 aspect ratio
  • 1080 by 1920 resolution
  • Audio track present
  • Duration between 8 and 90 seconds
  • Scene count in range
